Meet Ayfer

A colorist's eye, a sculptor's hand.

Before Artora, Ayfer owned and ran Ayfer Salon on Closter Dock Road in Closter. She created it as a refuge from a frazzled world, devoted to the lost art of pampering. Along the way she styled hair for models and runway shows, and her color work appeared in People magazine.

Artora Beauty Bar is the next chapter: a personal, light-filled studio in Tenafly. Ayfer is a lifelong student of fashion, art and technique. She keeps learning the newest cutting and color methods, and she still believes the best hair is made for real life, not just for the moment you leave the chair.

Styling philosophy

Personal, dimensional, and made to last.

Whether you want a cutting-edge look or a timeless classic, the goal is hair that reflects who you are and still looks beautiful weeks after your visit.

We listen first

Your bone structure, features and natural coloring, combined with your own vision, shape every cut and every formula. Nothing is one-size-fits-all.

Color with dimension

Warm golds, rich browns, buttery blondes and soft pastels, mixed by hand. The result is color with depth and shine that never reads flat.

A plan for healthy hair

Not sure how to get the look you want? We build a plan together. You'll leave with advice for keeping your hair healthy and vibrant between visits.

Meet Ayfer

A colorist's eye, a sculptor's hand.

“Ayfer Deniz believes in the transformative power of updating one's hair color, making life more colorful with brilliant dimension and shine.”

Before founding Artora, Ayfer owned and operated the beloved Ayfer Salon on Closter Dock Road in Closter. She envisioned it as a sanctuary from a frazzled world, dedicated to the lost art of pampering. Along the way, she styled hair for models and fashion runway shows, and her dimensional color artistry was featured in People magazine.

Artora Beauty Bar is her next chapter: a calm, light-filled second-floor studio in Tenafly. A lifelong student of fashion, art, and cutting technique, Ayfer continues mastering modern methods while ensuring every look is engineered for real life.
